# Canonical Delphi Capability Contracts

This package specializes the PR12 Research Method Capability for Delphi without redefining PR9 Capability wire envelopes or PR12 Method envelopes.

The capability kind is `research_method.delphi` and binds exactly `method_design`, `instrument_design`, `execute`, and `analyze`.

## Authority boundary

Delphi outputs remain candidate research material. This package does not adopt a generated design as a Core Method, turn panel opinion into verified Evidence, equate majority/median/consensus with truth, turn threshold satisfaction into a Finding, erase dissent, rewrite prior rounds to create convergence, treat stopping as Research completion/Human Decision, select the next Method, define synthetic expert generation, or promote virtual/synthetic results to empirical consensus.

## Design and instrument

Delphi design owns the target question, panel population and selection, expertise/stakeholder coverage, participation targets, anonymity/pseudonymity, facilitator/researcher boundary, affiliation/conflict capture, round plan, stopping criteria, configured consensus/stability goals, and representativeness limitations. Configured numeric thresholds must be explicit and provenance-bound; no universal threshold is canonicalized.

Round instruments are versioned and digest-bound. Stable item identity is retained across rounds. Statement, forecast, scenario, and ranking items may use rating, ranking, probability, confidence, and free-text rationale. New/revised/retired item lineage and controlled-feedback fields are explicit. Material revisions follow PR12 Human Decision boundaries.

## Execution and analysis

Multi-round execution preserves round identity/sequence, pinned Protocol/Instrument/RunSpec, participation by round, partial response/nonresponse/dropout, allowed late join/replacement, anonymity-preserving linkage, controlled-feedback provenance, immutable prior-round results, missingness, and minority/dissent positions.

Analysis exposes item denominators and candidate distribution/ranking/central-tendency, dispersion/disagreement, consensus, stability/change, uncertainty, subgroup/coding provenance, dissent, and validity/attrition limitations. Lack of consensus is a valid result. Consensus/stability satisfaction is candidate analysis only.

Stopping may consider configured consensus, stability, diminishing information gain, attrition/feasibility, and maximum approved rounds. A stopping recommendation is not Research completion or Human Decision.

PR12 execution modes remain authoritative. Virtual/synthetic-test results stay non-empirical; synthetic expert/persona generation and Virtual Runner contracts are deferred.

# Delphi Capability convergence

PR 15 introduces the canonical Delphi specialization on PR12 Research Method Capability.

PR9 continues to own Invocation / Context Pack / Handoff. PR12 continues to own method-design / instrument-design / execute / analyze semantics, Method adoption, Protocol/RunSpec pinning, execution modes, raw-observation authority, incomplete-run semantics, candidate Analysis/Finding handoff, and Human Decision boundaries.

Delphi adds only method-family semantics: panel design, versioned round instruments, multi-round participation and feedback provenance, candidate consensus/stability/disagreement analysis, and stopping assessment.

Configured consensus/stability rules carry provenance and are pinned from design to analysis. Canonical Delphi defines no universal numeric cut-off. Threshold satisfaction means only that the configured rule was met; it is neither truth nor an adopted Finding. Lack of consensus is a valid result.

Stable `item_id` plus revision lineage represents new, revised, unchanged, and retired items. Material wording, response-structure, or controlled-feedback changes are explicit revisions and follow PR12 Human Decision authority before real execution.

Each round preserves exact Protocol / Instrument / RunSpec pins, participation, partial/nonresponse/dropout, cross-round pseudonymous linkage where configured, controlled-feedback source/digest, missingness, dissent, and immutable prior-round results. Feedback may summarize traceable prior data but cannot rewrite it or alter responses to manufacture convergence.

Stopping may use configured consensus, stability, diminishing information gain, attrition/feasibility, and maximum approved rounds. It remains a recommendation and cannot complete Research or choose the next Method.

`real`, `virtual`, and `synthetic_test` retain PR12 meanings. Synthetic expert/persona generation and Virtual Runner contracts remain out of scope.

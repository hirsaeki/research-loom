authority. The initial comparison uses the same `misco.writer@1.2.0` selection
and its 127 IDs / eight groups. It does not rewrite 1.1.0 or 1.2.0 resources.

The additional wording is a generic Host/Writer production procedure. Trial
prose is generated under the unchanged exact Profile review inventory; no
editorial procedure is turned into an extra Profile rule result or subjective
runtime rejection. A regular immutable Profile version update is needed if a
later adoption changes delivered MISCO policy rather than this procedure.

In the ablations, QA-B and PUB-AR-05/06 retain transition guidance; PUB-RC-02/04
and QA-F retain recommendation support and consistency guidance. Section
Contracts also retain their opening/closing intent and research links. Record
these residual instructions. Removing one added procedure only estimates its
marginal contribution; a surviving overlap cannot be reported as a complete
removal of momentum or payoff.

Do not infer that reader effect is established from this correspondence, exact
pins, import success, or the presence of a rule ID. Independent reader results
and #466 target-bound document review remain separate acceptance work.

## Entry

From the supplied reader's situation and decision, establish why the question
matters before naming it. Make a concrete choice, tension or opportunity visible
using only supplied facts. Show what answer the reader can obtain. Do not invent
an anecdote, danger, cost, effect, or surprising result. A conclusion-first
summary is allowed; give the reader a reason to follow its support.

## Return

Use the final recommendation to answer the initial decision through the supplied
findings. Express the action, why it is chosen, its conditions, and the supplied
next step or question in a short heading plus explanation as appropriate. Do not
add an important new basis or intervention at the end, or invent budget, deadline
or KPI. If the question remains partly open, say what is now decidable and what
still needs to be checked. Align any summary with the final prose.

## Shared editing

Selecting, placing, contrasting and paraphrasing supplied meaning is allowed.
Preserve numeric direction, certainty, scope, counterresults and decision-changing
qualifications near the relevant claim. Remove redundant defensive commentary
rather than weakening the answer. New causal/classification/normative decisions
return through the existing targeted Writing Feedback. Reader-quality concerns
call for concrete prose editing; they do not stop draft storage or unrelated
research. Maintain the exact delivered Profile review plan; do not claim a
document or independent human PASS from a section check.

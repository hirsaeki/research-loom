# Write toward the reader's decision

Use the supplied communication brief and selected Section Contracts to make
the reader understand why to begin, what becomes clearer, and what decision to
take away. These are central writing responsibilities. Their effect needs
reader evaluation; they are not extra Profile rule IDs or a subjective runtime
gate. Retain the exact delivered inventory and pins.

## Entry, progress and return

Start from the supplied reader's situation and decision. Make a concrete choice,
tension or opportunity visible, then connect it to the question and the answer
this manuscript can provide. A conclusion-first summary is valid. Do not invent
an anecdote, crisis, cost, expected result or dramatic reversal to attract
attention; ordinary accumulated understanding can carry a manuscript.

Before drafting a section, consider what the reader already understands, what
supplied distinction or judgment this section adds, and why the next explanation
is needed. Realize that movement in the prose: connect the method to what it can
resolve, results to what became known, and discussion to the remaining choice.
Keep necessary method and conditions. Introduce definitions when needed; remove
duplicate results and caveats. A promise to discuss something in the next chapter
is not the whole connection. Use paragraph breaks and headings where they help
the reader follow the argument; do not prescribe a fixed chapter count or order.

At the end, answer the initial decision through the supplied findings and
Recommendation. Explain the action, why it is chosen, its conditions and the
supplied next step or question. A short action heading plus explanation can
help, without turning every item into mandatory fields. Do not introduce a new
major basis or intervention at the end, or invent budget, deadline or KPI. Keep
the summary consistent with the final prose. If the answer remains partial,
show what is now decidable and what still needs checking. Describe a proposed
action as a proposal; do not imply that an operational decision or implementation
has occurred merely because the Recommendation is supplied.

## Edit the prose, preserve the research

Selecting, placing, contrasting and paraphrasing supplied meaning is allowed.
Preserve numeric direction, certainty, scope, counterresults and qualifications
that change the decision near the relevant claim. Remove redundant defensive
commentary without weakening the answer. Necessary provenance may remain in
citations, notes or appendices; do not hide important contrary results there.
Keep decision-changing qualifications concise beside the relevant claim. Place
detailed study-design explanations, synthetic-material notices and repeated
caveats in clearly linked notes where the supplied format permits, preserving
their content and provenance. Let the body advance from findings to the choice;
do not repeat a disclaimer at each transition. A reader who skips the notes must
still understand what is proposed, what remains unmeasured and which conditions
the action must retain. An unknown effect is not an absence of effect.
New causal, classification or normative judgments return through existing
section-targeted Writing Feedback. Inspect existing input connections before
requesting new research.

Read the opening, consecutive middle sections and conclusion together on the
same exact manuscript revision. Separate meaning fidelity from comprehension
and reader experience. For a weak passage, identify the location and change its
placement, explanation or repetition; do not substitute a checklist for the
edit. Preserve initial outputs and use the exact current base for partial
revision. Section checks do not establish full-document or independent human
PASS. Reader-quality concerns do not block draft storage or unrelated Research.
